# Hayley — Asset View Guide

This part of the Dashboard helps you see what New Earth owns and what might affect money decisions.

You do not need to manage every technical detail.

## Focus on

- things needing receipts
- things needing a spending decision
- things needing reorder
- broken items needing replacement
- items bought but not registered

## Simple status meanings

- 🟢 Available
- 🟡 Low stock / watch
- 🔴 Broken or stop using
- 🔵 Needs decision
- 🟣 Wishlist / future
