# FSD — Valuation, Insurance and Business Value

## Purpose

Asset Intelligence supports business valuation by proving what New Earth owns, what it cost, what it may be worth, what is used in R&D, what belongs to each project, and what would need replacing after loss or damage.

## Valuation categories

- Physical assets
- Tools and lab equipment
- Inventory and stock
- Prototype/R&D equipment
- Project-specific parts
- Insurance replacement value

## Dashboard summary

Show:

- purchase cost total
- replacement value total
- current estimated value
- asset value by project
- broken/lost value
- insured/uninsured status later

## Business valuation rule

Cost is not always value.

Receipts prove cost.  
Asset records prove ownership.  
Valuation notes explain business value.
