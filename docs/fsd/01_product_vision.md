1. Product Vision
The New Earth Command Dashboard is a personal mission-control app designed to help manage, build, and organise the many moving parts of New Earth.
The app will bring together projects, business tasks, learning, content creation, daily planning, wellbeing, and long-term vision into one clear working dashboard.
The purpose is to make New Earth easier to manage, easier to build, and easier to stay aligned with each day.
Rather than feeling scattered across different notebooks, folders, apps, websites, devices, and ideas, the dashboard becomes the central place where Peter can see:
•	what matters today 
•	what projects are active 
•	what tasks need doing 
•	what learning is required 
•	what business actions need attention 
•	what content should be created 
•	what progress has been made 
•	what needs to happen next 
The app is not just a productivity tool. It is a New Earth operating system for daily action.
________________________________________
2. Core Purpose
The core purpose of the app is to help answer one question every day:
What should I focus on today to move New Earth forward?
The app should reduce overwhelm by turning many different ideas, tasks, and projects into a clear daily structure.
It should help organise:
•	MicroGrow 
•	MicroGrow Field Scanner 
•	New Earth website 
•	New Earth Living app 
•	Smart Growing Systems book 
•	LinkedIn and public awareness 
•	business and funding 
•	learning and skill development 
•	hardware/software development 
•	personal wellbeing 
•	future project ideas 
The dashboard should support both practical building and deeper alignment with the New Earth vision.
________________________________________
3. Problem Statement
New Earth is growing into a large multi-project vision.
There are many active areas happening at the same time:
•	engineering 
•	embedded systems 
•	app development 
•	website building 
•	writing 
•	social media 
•	business planning 
•	funding 
•	learning 
•	community awareness 
•	personal development 
Because the vision is large, it can become difficult to know what to focus on first.
Important tasks can get mixed together with ideas, learning notes, website updates, business plans, book content, and future concepts.
This creates several problems:
3.1 Overwhelm
There are too many moving parts to hold in memory at once.
3.2 Lack of daily focus
Without a clear daily command centre, it becomes easy to jump between tasks without knowing which ones matter most.
3.3 Scattered information
Tasks, ideas, notes, designs, content, and plans may be spread across different places.
3.4 Difficulty tracking progress
When work is happening across multiple projects, it can be hard to see how much has actually been achieved.
3.5 Vision drift
Some tasks may feel urgent but may not be aligned with the bigger New Earth mission.
3.6 Lost build history
The daily journey of building New Earth contains valuable lessons, updates, and stories that could become website journal posts, LinkedIn content, book material, and project documentation.
________________________________________
4. Proposed Solution
Create a dashboard app that acts as a daily operating system for New Earth.
The app will organise work into clear sections:
•	daily dashboard 
•	projects 
•	tasks 
•	weekly planner 
•	learning tracker 
•	business hub 
•	content planner 
•	build journal 
•	vision alignment checker 
•	wellbeing check-in 
The app should make it simple to open the dashboard in the morning and know exactly what to do next.
The first version should be simple, practical, and usable quickly.
The app should not start as a complex enterprise system. It should begin as a focused personal dashboard that helps Peter manage the daily build of New Earth.
________________________________________
5. Product Goals
Goal 1 — Create daily clarity
The app should show the main focus for the day and the top three tasks that matter most.
Goal 2 — Reduce overwhelm
The app should organise ideas, tasks, learning, and project work into clear categories.
Goal 3 — Track New Earth projects
The app should provide a project hub for all active New Earth projects.
Goal 4 — Support build consistency
The app should help create a daily rhythm of planning, doing, reviewing, and improving.
Goal 5 — Connect work to vision
The app should help check whether tasks are aligned with the New Earth Blueprint and long-term mission.
Goal 6 — Capture progress
The app should make it easy to record what was built, learned, tested, fixed, or published each day.
Goal 7 — Support learning
The app should track learning topics that directly support the mission, such as Flutter, ESP32, STM32, AI, cybersecurity, WordPress, electronics, and business.
Goal 8 — Support business growth
The app should help organise funding opportunities, job applications, contacts, outreach, offers, and business actions.
Goal 9 — Support content creation
The app should capture ideas for LinkedIn posts, website journal entries, videos, project updates, and book content.
Goal 10 — Protect personal energy
The app should include wellbeing checks so that the work remains sustainable.
________________________________________
6. Non-Goals for Version 0.1
The first version should not try to do everything.
For V0.1, the app should not focus on:
•	complex team collaboration 
•	full accounting 
•	advanced AI automation 
•	cloud sync 
•	public user accounts 
•	marketplace features 
•	complex permission systems 
•	full CRM functionality 
•	deep calendar integration 
•	automated website publishing 
•	advanced analytics 
These can come later.
The first version should focus on making Peter’s working day clearer and easier to manage.

8. User Needs
The user needs to:
•	know what to work on today 
•	avoid feeling lost across too many projects 
•	track active tasks 
•	store future ideas without distraction 
•	manage learning 
•	plan LinkedIn and website content 
•	keep MicroGrow moving forward 
•	manage business/funding actions 
•	record daily progress 
•	connect work back to the New Earth vision 
•	stay balanced and not burn out 
________________________________________
9. Main Success Criteria
The app will be successful if it helps the user:
1. Open the app each morning and know what matters today.
2. Choose three clear priority tasks.
3. Track project progress without confusion.
4. Capture ideas instead of losing them.
5. Review what was achieved at the end of the day.
6. Turn daily progress into content, documentation, or planning.
7. Feel more organised and less overwhelmed.
8. Keep New Earth moving forward consistently.
________________________________________
10. Product Statement
The New Earth Command Dashboard is a personal mission-control app that helps Peter organise his working day across projects, business, learning, content, and wellbeing.
It exists to make New Earth easier to manage, easier to build, and easier to grow by turning a large vision into clear daily action.
________________________________________
11. MVP Vision Statement
The first version of the app should be simple:
A dashboard that shows today’s focus, top three tasks, active projects, learning focus, content focus, and end-of-day review.
The MVP should help Peter move from:
“I have too much going on and do not know where to start.”
to:
“I know exactly what I am working on today and why it matters.”
________________________________________
12. Guiding Principle
The app should always be designed around this principle:
Clarity first. Complexity later.
The dashboard must not become another overwhelming system.
Every feature should make the working day easier, not heavier.
