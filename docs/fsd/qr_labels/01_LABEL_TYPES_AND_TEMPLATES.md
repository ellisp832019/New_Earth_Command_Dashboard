# FSD — Label Types and Templates

## Label types

### Asset label

For individual equipment/assets.

```text
NE-TOOL-0001
Soldering Iron
QR
```

### Parts label

For parts bins and components.

```text
NE-PART-0008
ESP32-S3 Boards
Qty: 12
QR
```

### Bin label

For drawers, boxes and shelves.

```text
BIN-MG-004
MicroGrow Sensors
QR
```

### Cable label

For USB cables, power cables, adapters.

```text
NE-CABLE-0003
USB-C Data Cable
QR
```

### Maintenance tag

For broken/repair items.

```text
REPAIR
NE-EQ-0004
Do not use
QR
```

### Reorder point label

Placed inside bins.

```text
REORDER WHEN BELOW 5
JST-XH Connectors
QR
```

## Recommended label sizes

- 20x10mm: tiny parts, cables
- 30x20mm: ESP32 boards, sensors
- 40x30mm: tools, small boxes
- 50x30mm: equipment
- 50x50mm: bins and shelves
- 60x40mm: maintenance/reorder labels
