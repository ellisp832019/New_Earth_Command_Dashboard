# Rejection Learning Loop

Rejected grants are not failures. They are training data.

After every rejection, complete:

1. What did the funder say?
2. Was the project eligible?
3. Was the evidence weak?
4. Was the budget weak?
5. Was the partner case weak?
6. Was the innovation unclear?
7. Was the impact unclear?
8. What text can be reused?
9. What must be improved before the next application?

Then update the readiness score rules if needed.
