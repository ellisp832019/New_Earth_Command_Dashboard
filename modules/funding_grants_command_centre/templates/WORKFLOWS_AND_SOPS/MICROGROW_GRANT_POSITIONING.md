# MicroGrow Grant Positioning

## Strongest positioning

MicroGrow is a local-first smart growing automation system for education, food resilience, small-scale growers and rehabilitation settings.

## Avoid

Do not pitch the full New Earth vision first.

## Use this instead

A bounded technical prototype and pilot-readiness project.

## Best first funding ask

£10,000 to £25,000 for:

- prototype hardening
- PCB V0.1
- enclosure prototype
- app polish
- diagnostics
- technical documentation
- evidence pack
- pilot-readiness testing

## Strong phrases

- local-first
- safe low-voltage prototype
- education-ready
- food resilience
- practical skills
- environmental monitoring
- controlled environment agriculture
- modular open architecture
- privacy-preserving
- community-scale innovation
