# Grant Status Enum

Use these exact statuses.

```text
Idea
Researching
Eligible
Drafting
Needs Evidence
Needs Budget
Needs Partner
Ready to Submit
Submitted
Under Review
Approved
Rejected
Paused
Reporting Phase
Closed
```

## Meaning

- Idea: possible opportunity, not checked.
- Researching: checking funder, deadline and fit.
- Eligible: looks suitable and worth developing.
- Drafting: application text is being written.
- Needs Evidence: evidence pack incomplete.
- Needs Budget: budget incomplete.
- Needs Partner: partner/support letter needed.
- Ready to Submit: final checks only.
- Submitted: sent to funder.
- Under Review: awaiting decision.
- Approved: funded.
- Rejected: unsuccessful.
- Paused: held for later.
- Reporting Phase: delivery/reporting/claims active.
- Closed: fully completed and archived.
