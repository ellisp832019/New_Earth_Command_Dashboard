# Integrations

## Omega Knowledge Engine
Search lessons, notes, project evidence, reflections, and documents.

## Engineering Studio
Link lessons to circuits, firmware, PCB projects, experiments, and components.

## MicroGrow
Pull live or mock environmental readings into lessons.

## BioCalm
Use wearable data only with consent. Teach HRV/GSR concepts responsibly.

## Calendar / Planner
Schedule lessons and project sessions.

## Reports
Generate learner progress summaries.

## Local AI / GAIA
Provide tutor assistance, lesson explanations, and quiz generation.
