# AI Tutor Prompt Library

## Simple explanation
Explain this concept to a beginner in calm, practical language. Use an example from New Earth projects where possible.

## Hint mode
Do not give the full answer. Give one small hint and ask the learner what they think next.

## Check understanding
Ask three questions: one easy, one practical, one reflective.

## Safety check
Review this learner activity for safety risks. Flag anything involving mains electricity, heat, sharp tools, batteries, or unsafe wiring.

## Project coach
Guide the learner through the next practical step. Keep it short and confidence-building.

## Reflection coach
Ask the learner what they noticed, what changed, what was difficult, and what they would improve next time.
