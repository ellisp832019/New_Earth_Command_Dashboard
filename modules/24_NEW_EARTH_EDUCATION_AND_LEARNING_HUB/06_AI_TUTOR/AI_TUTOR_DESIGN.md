# AI Tutor Design

## Purpose
Support learners with clear explanations, hints, practice questions, and reflection prompts.

## Modes
- Explain simply
- Give me a hint
- Check my answer
- Quiz me
- Help me reflect
- Help me build safely
- Show me next steps

## Guardrails
- Do not encourage unsafe mains electrical work.
- Do not give harmful instructions.
- For minors, use supportive and age-appropriate language.
- Encourage asking a trusted adult for practical tool use.
- Avoid pretending to be a human teacher or therapist.
- Encourage hands-on evidence and reflection.

## Local-first AI
Prefer local LLM via GAIA/Ollama for privacy. Internet-based AI should be optional and clearly marked.
