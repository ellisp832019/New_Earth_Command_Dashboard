# Sample Lesson: LED, Resistor and Safe Current

## Summary
Learn why an LED needs a resistor and how current flows in a simple circuit.

## Learning outcomes
- Identify LED polarity
- Explain current limiting
- Build a simple LED circuit
- Record what happened

## Materials
- Breadboard
- LED
- 220Ω resistor
- Jumper wires
- 3.3V or 5V supply

## Safety notes
Use low voltage only. Do not connect directly to mains electricity.

## Activity
1. Place the LED on the breadboard.
2. Add the resistor in series.
3. Connect to safe low voltage power.
4. Observe brightness.
5. Reverse the LED and observe what changes.

## Reflection
What did the resistor protect? What happened when the LED direction changed?
