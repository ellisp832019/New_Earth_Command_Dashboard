# Learning Pathways Overview

## Pathway 1: Electronics Foundations
Basics of voltage, current, resistance, breadboards, LEDs, sensors, relays, power, safety.

## Pathway 2: Embedded Systems
ESP32, Arduino-style coding, GPIO, I2C, SPI, BLE, Wi-Fi basics, firmware structure.

## Pathway 3: MicroGrow Operator
Food growing, environment control, sensors, irrigation, lighting, grow recipes, maintenance.

## Pathway 4: BioCalm Foundations
Nervous system basics, HRV, GSR, breath, wearables, ethical wellbeing technology.

## Pathway 5: AI Literacy
What AI is, local AI, prompt skills, responsible use, hallucinations, privacy.

## Pathway 6: Food Resilience
Seeds, microgreens, growing cycles, nutrition, community food systems.

## Pathway 7: Youth Innovation & Leadership
Ideas, teamwork, animals and care projects, social impact, presentation skills.

## Pathway 8: Workshop Safety
Tools, soldering safety, electrical boundaries, PPE, risk assessment.

## Pathway 9: Business & Enterprise Basics
Problem solving, value, customers, simple finance, pitch practice.

## Pathway 10: Systems Thinking
How parts connect, feedback loops, inputs/outputs, resilience, failure modes.
