# Learner Safety

## Safety principles
- Low-voltage education first
- No mains electrical instruction for unsupervised learners
- Clear adult supervision warnings
- Tool safety notices
- Battery safety notices
- Privacy-first learner data
- Local storage by default

## AI safety
The AI tutor should refuse unsafe practical instructions and redirect to safe alternatives.

## Wellbeing
Reflections should be supportive, not diagnostic. The module should not claim to provide therapy or medical assessment.
