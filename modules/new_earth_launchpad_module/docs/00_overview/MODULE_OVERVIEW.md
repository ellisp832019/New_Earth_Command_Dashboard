# Module Overview — New Earth Launchpad

## Purpose

New Earth Launchpad is the campaign and venture operating system for taking New Earth projects into the world.

It is intentionally separate from MicroGrow so it can later support:

- MicroGrow Kickstarter
- BioCalm wearable development campaign
- New Earth Living launch
- Prison rehabilitation pilot funding
- Grants
- Investor outreach
- Partner pilots
- Community-backed projects

## Core Modules

1. Campaign Manager
2. Story Builder
3. Prototype Readiness Tracker
4. Financial Model
5. Reward Manager
6. Media Studio
7. Manufacturing Planner
8. Risk Register
9. Community Builder
10. Grants Centre
11. Investor / Partner CRM
12. Launch Checklist
13. Backer Communications
14. Fulfilment Tracker
15. Impact Tracker

## First Practical Use

The first real use case is MicroGrow Kickstarter 2026.

The job of the module is to answer:

- Are we ready to launch?
- What proof do we have?
- What are we asking for?
- What will backers receive?
- What will it cost us to deliver?
- What risks must be disclosed?
- What content still needs creating?
- What must happen before launch day?
